LESSON 1 / 3

Motivation vs Discipline

Motivation is the reason or feeling that helps you begin. Discipline is the practice of following a chosen system even when motivation is low.

Af-Soomaali: Motivation waa dhiirrigelinta bilowga; Discipline waa nidaamka iyo ficilka joogtada ah ee qofku raaco.

Layli / Exercise

Qor hal hadaf oo aad leedahay, kadibna u beddel ficil yar oo aad ku celin karto maalin kasta.
